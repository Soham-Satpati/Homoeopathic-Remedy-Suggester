HoRUS runs entirely on this computer. Nothing is sent over the internet.

What gets installed
  - The HoRUS app
  - The remedy dataset
  - Offline AI models: a language model for reading cases and writing explanations,
    a speech model for dictation, and a symptom-matching model (about 4 GB in total)

Requirements
  - Windows 10 (1809) or Windows 11, 64-bit
  - 8 GB RAM recommended (4 GB works with the AI switched off)
  - About 6 GB of free disk space

Patient records are kept encrypted in your Windows user profile and are not removed if
HoRUS is uninstalled. Back them up from Settings -> Backups.

For clinical reference only. Prescribing decisions rest with the practitioner.
